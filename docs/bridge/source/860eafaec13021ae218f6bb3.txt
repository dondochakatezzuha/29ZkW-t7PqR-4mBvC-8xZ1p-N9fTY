38.11 BOSS POWERS AND TEAM APPLICATIONS

The new boss register lists every named card, including the twin characters, rather than treating Kaizen as the story's central character. Astavia/Mother's card gives three eyes, a void core, seven crown fragments, four arms, reality-window/fragment design labels and a Dragon++++ rating. Rerar has a time orb and a statement that time bends to his will. Calli and Kalli each have Reality, Illusion and Spirit Manipulation and Dragon+ ratings. Dull 'The Giver' has Dragon+++++. These labels are usable encounter information, but are not complete move sheets or proof of unlimited effects. The other cards mostly establish names, roles and ratings.

The following are team-planning deductions, not additional grants or already demonstrated combinations.

Kaizen's retained '1' offers spatial routes, witnessed-impact storage, physical-history restoration, hollow-space traps and delayed consequences. Void adds creations, a separate dimension, detection and gradual energy-specific adaptation. The two toolkits could coordinate positioning and stored attacks while Null/cell defenses sustain pressure, but no source supplies a multiplicative stacking formula or says delay prevents all costs. The former Winter and Dominion should not remain in this current combination.

Toby now contributes Aevor's temporal controls and Void alongside his separately sourced spirit/class/Titan kit. Replace old Crown/Zhaevir eye tactics when planning his equipped loadout. Dargan's Secret Eye 3 network can support team communication and connection analysis, Director redirects traveling attacks with its stated bypass, and Void offers another spatial/energy toolset. Shared information does not automatically share ownership of eyes or private system rewards.

Loki's OVRATH diagnoses and transfers energy, Secret Eye 1 develops through processing/progression and Sun supplies solar offense/defense. The eight-stone lock remains even if a teammate can analyze energy. Adelram's '1'/Moon/Secret Eye 2 set combines spatial and reflected-attack options with progression, property exchange and one-rule exceptions. That does not establish a completed hand-eye enhancement or every rule being bypassable for free.

Arlo's portal/gravity/liquid control supports positioning and containment; Mariam's stellar pressure and endurance support sustained attacks; Monarch's Genesis/Astralux flexibility offers scouting, mobility and changing class combinations. Their documented strengths matter even below the top four in the dated ranking. Exact Jerial techniques and rare Slug availability must be established before relying on them. Alay's confirmed eye should be tracked without inventing a whole class kit.

Against the previewed reality, illusion and spirit powers, coordinate sensory checks, communication, spatial exits, effect timing and energy costs. No posted boss label guarantees which defense wins. Autonomous reflections, tendrils, poison and area attacks lack a blanket ally-safety rule. Player merge carries both buffs and nerfs; decide contributions and ARO burdens explicitly. Existing Spirit Realm, Yellow Stone air-to-water and Mercury Null distinctions remain in force unless an actual later ruling changes them.
