# WELCOME TO REGNUM OF REGALIA
**Please read and follow our server rules to keep the game running smoothly.**

**Section 1: Server Conduct**

**1. Zero Tolerance for Arguing**
The GM’s ruling is final. Accept action results and story beats. You may DM the GM after the game to discuss issues peacefully, but arguing in active channels is strictly prohibited.

**2. Watch Your Language**
Cursing is fine in-character. However, excessive cursing, targeted toxicity, or harassment will not be tolerated.

**3. No Scam Links or Spam**
No unauthorized links, self-promotion, or scam material. Keep this space purely for the game.

**Section 2: How We Play**

**4. Action Declaration Only**
Type only what your character attempts to do. You cannot dictate outcomes, control other players, or auto-hit enemies.

**5. The GM Tells the Story**
State your intent and wait. The GM rolls hidden checks and types out the narrative outcome.

**6. Bold Text for Story Actions**
All story actions, in-character dialogue, and narrative text must be written in bold. Regular text is reserved strictly for out-of-character (OOC) chatter, questions, and mechanics.

**Section 3: Activity & Disciplinary Action**

**7. Inactivity = Kick**
Text RPGs die without momentum. If you hold up the game without notifying the GM of an absence, you will be kicked so the group can continue.

**8. Punishment Tiers**

    **Muted:** Minor offenses (borderline excessive swearing).
    **Kicked:** Repeated minor offenses, arguing with the GM, or unexcused inactivity.
    **Banned:** Immediate removal for severe infractions (scam links, extreme toxicity, malicious behavior).

**React below to confirm you have read, understood, and agreed to these rules!**
