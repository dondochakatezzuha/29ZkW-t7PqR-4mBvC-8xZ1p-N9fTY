8. Loki: Sanctuary, family relocation and system wealth

Aviala's recently aged child is explicitly confirmed to be a girl and named Athena, age five. This corrects the earlier 'son' wording. Loki visits Kaizen's family; Athena and Laja play. Lust subsequently escorts Paja, Soren and Laja through Shadow Domain to a castle. At 10:58 Lust also arrives with Toby's family and is instructed to settle them in their own castle. The scene does not enumerate every arriving person or resolve Toby's later request to speak with Artemis; do not infer an identity list from 'everyone'. Liana and Aviala are alive and waiting when Loki returns with the children.

K uses Loki's Primal to create Primal Sanctuary, a private training dimension with arenas, clones and an Athena play area. K claims only authorized family can enter and even Shift needs permission, but no independent intrusion test establishes that absolute protection. Headbands identify Maeve in blue, Damien in red and Azreal in yellow.

Two continuity conflicts remain visible. The Sanctuary initially disables abilities, yet later scenes use staffs, shadows and teleportation without an explicit rule change. Loki's earlier Primal/Stones/peak seal is also not explicitly removed before K uses Primal here. Preserve the observed actions but do not silently declare all powers unsealed or the no-powers rule permanently abolished. Loki later reiterates that his Dragon x2 billion strength was sealed.

K reports Loki's Outside System balance as 250 million OM: 150 million for reaching Dragon x2 billion and 100 million from other completed achievements. This is an account/achievement panel, not an unsealing or new active strength grant. No transfer of this money or TGC eye occurs despite offers and player banter.

9. Damien, Azreal, Maeve and Athena

Azreal wins the first physical round against Damien. Damien subsequently shows blue eyes during improved blocking/strategy; no new numbered eye or Cell is identified. Staff transformations/clones, levitation and feints occur, while Azreal uses shadow intangibility. K estimates Damien's IQ as 2665 and Azreal's as 3372: these are in-fiction system estimates, not real psychological measurements. A staff collision explodes across the arena; both boys remain conscious with minor injuries. Varkhazar emerges from Loki's skin to shield Maeve and Athena.

Azreal's green eyes activate an automatic defence against Loki's attacks. K attributes it to Aviala's bloodline as former Queen of the North; the exact ability name remains unknown. This is not an award of GreenCell, Time Stone or an identified TGC eye. Tests show attacks fast enough to exceed his reactions can bypass it, and Loki stops before impact. The eyes return to normal. Deliberate activation and complete invulnerability are not established.

Maeve first struggles with stance, punches and teleportation and becomes discouraged. After Loki asks K to train her, the 10:41 scene shows an effective combination: teleport behind Loki, staff strike, Shadow Form through his body, punch, then swap positions with her staff and kick while K controls Loki's body. K says she outsmarted him using techniques learned minutes earlier. This is successful training and a specific landed combination, not proof she is stronger than every character. The actual result supersedes her initial inability. Her staff, teleportation, Shadow Form and position-swap belong in her own entry.

Athena remains a five-year-old child participating in the family scenes and cheering on Maeve. No full premium manipulation set or new combat feat is awarded to her here. Loki's claim that his children are strongest in the entire realm is a player's boast. General jokes about numerous wives/children or universal Null adulthood rules are not a canon census or a proven race mechanic.

10. Shift family lore and TGC versus Vespa eyes

Draven identifies five children of the NPC Shift: Albion, Move, run, Mercer and Estelar. This refers to the story entity, not automatically the Discord user currently named Shift who plays Loki. Draven also explains that Move's father chose his large appearance and could change it but does not want to. These are attributed lore explanations, not a new player race or power acquisition.

Draven says only three TGC Eyes exist in all TGC. A separate statement calls Vespa a TGC-ranked entity while saying its eyes are not ranked in that way. A forwarded historical Eye of Vespa listing from General gives RoR rarity, Dragon requirement, trade through Draven and a 1.2 trillion VIP-card price; Draven answers that it is RoR. Preserve the distinction between the entity's rank, the historical item's rarity and the three numbered TGC Eyes. The repost is not a current purchase, new owner or proof that RoR and TGC eyes are identical.
