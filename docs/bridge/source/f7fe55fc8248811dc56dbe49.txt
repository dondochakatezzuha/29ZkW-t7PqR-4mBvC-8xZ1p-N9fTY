**NEW CLASS : ECHO**

Echo is an Aro Class that lets the user leave a visible ARO afterimage of one physical movement and replay it once after a short delay. The echo begins where the original movement happened and follows the exact same path, direction and reach. It can repeat a punch, kick or weapon swing, giving the user a delayed follow-up while they move into a different position.

Only one movement can be stored at a time. The user chooses the delay when creating it, up to three seconds, and cannot change its path afterward. Each replay costs ARO and cannot hit harder than the original movement. It copies the motion and physical strike, not another class ability, a weapon's special effects, a teleport or an energy blast. An echo cannot make more echoes and disappears after its single replay. It is not a separate person and cannot think, chase, adapt or automatically defend the user.

For example, the user swings across an opponent's guard, steps to the side, then the echo repeats that swing through the original space a moment later. The opponent can dodge out of that path, block it or pressure the user before they set up another one. Echo rewards timing, positioning and setting traps; a badly placed movement just gets repeated badly.
