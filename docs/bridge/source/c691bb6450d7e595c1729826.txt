37.2 EYE MARKET, ALTERS, SPIN LEDGER AND TITAN REQUEST

Croco introduces trading, sales for spins and Alters. Eye trades require matching value: his example for Dragon+ is three Mythicals, two Mythical++ and one Mythical+++. Preserve this as the stated example; it is not a complete conversion table for every rarity. Items received from another player through trading become 'Alters' and cannot then be sold. This prevents assuming an unrestricted trade-and-resell loop. No actual eye transplant or physical transfer procedure is supplied here.

The initial sell announcement quotes two spins below Legendary, 35 above Mythical+++ and 155 for Dragon+++, with batches allowed but a 24-hour-or-longer wait afterward depending on amount sold. Later, Croco quotes three spins below Legendary, 35 above Mythical+++ and 155 above Dragon+++. Actual rulings give Adelram two spins for Zyra and a two-hour resell cooldown, and Arlo three for Ryth with a two-hour cooldown. Preserve both dated statements and executed outcomes. The threshold wording and 2-versus-3 payouts / 2-hour-versus-24-hour cooldowns are inconsistent; no single universal price or cooldown table is settled by this transcript.

Loki's actual Moon x2 + Dominion bundle pays 190 total. Do not independently price each Moon at 155 and override the GM's total. The recorded ledger is: 190 received; 70 spent for Sun; 45 for Secret Eye 1; 45 transferred to Adelram with GM acknowledgment; 12 for OVRATH. Arithmetic leaves 18 from those recorded transactions. Loki instead calls his pre-OVRATH balance 25 and later says 13 remain. That leaves a five-spin discrepancy unless another deduction is established. Keep the actual awards and the player-stated 13 balance; do not award extra spins based solely on arithmetic.

Adelram's transferred 45 are fully accounted for: 32 for Secret Eye 2 plus 13 for Moon. His earlier Zyra sale and subsequent rolls are a separate sequence. Dargan requests use of 12 remaining spins; Croco grants Void in six, without separately restating the final balance. Arlo's Ryth sale supplies three spent on Zyra, followed by two five-spin runs supplying Ire twice. Lock-in can skip a stronger result and finish below the requested tier.

Asked whether players can replace their Titans' eyes, Croco replies with a sticker whose visible text is 'noooo'. Record that negative response and the absence of any approved replacement; it is not a detailed transplant rule or a granted Titan upgrade. The earlier 'Demon Stare' sticker in response to a Secret Eye 3 request is not the full power sheet. The later five-part Secret Eye 3 description is the substantive source. Loki's plan for a Sun-related Cell Fuse form is likewise not an approved new form.
