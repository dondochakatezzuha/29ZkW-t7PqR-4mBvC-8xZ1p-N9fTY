## Text

ARLO: Ryth is sold for three spins. All three produce Zyra, which Croco calls Common. Croco then corrects his initial no-spins response: Arlo has five spins for each of the two other eyes that had not been spun. Both runs end on Ire; the final GM confirmation says all ten spins were used and he has two Ire eyes. Latest named set: Zyra plus Ire plus Ire, with anatomical sides unspecified. Ire is Uncommon. The second run requests Void and skips Sundered Crown, Mythical+; neither skipped Crown nor requested Void is an award to Arlo. The earlier Ryth result is superseded.

TOBY: no new sale or roll; retain Sundered Crown left (Mythical+) and Zhaevir right (Mythical+++), with two canonical eyes.
KAIZEN / 01yt / UMBRA: no new sale or roll; retain the eye literally named '1' left (Dragon+++) and Winter right (rarity still unstated). This '1' is not Loki's Secret Eye 1.
MARIAM: no new roll; retain the last Vexis/Thaeron grants, the unresolved older laterality labels and four canonical eyes with the other slots not filled by a new result.
MONARCH / HAMZA: no new eye roll; retain Thaeron left and Zyra right. His later Astralux/Genesis record and the user's earlier Yellow Stone, Space/Astria and Mystia sheet remain dated sources with coexistence questions preserved.

Croco states that acquired eyes are permanent unless lost in-game or stolen. This concerns persistence; it does not override the explicitly completed sales or replacements above, and threats to pluck an eye are not completed thefts.
