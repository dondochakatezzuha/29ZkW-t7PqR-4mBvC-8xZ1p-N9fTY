Croco resolves Loki's Slug demonstration: Adarlok loads a Slug Shell into the blaster, steps outside Amalia's cottage and fires into the sky. The Slug expands into a larger form and uses Air Breath, producing large spiralling tornadoes through the Sanctuary landscape. The winds shake trees and scatter leaves. After the attack fades, the creature shrinks and lands on Adarlok's shoulder. No victim injury or quantified range, launch velocity, cooldown or energy cost is stated. This demonstrates a launch-triggered transformation, an air attack, reversion and return; it does not establish every possible Slug's performance.

FULL DEMONSTRATION

Adarlok decided to show Amalia something, reaching for one of the Slug Shells he carried before revealing the small creature resting inside 

 Amalia leaned closer, her eyes lighting up with curiosity as Adarlok inserted the shell into his Slug Blaster 

 He stepped outside the cottage, raised the blaster towards the open sky and pulled the trigger 

 The slug shot through the air at incredible speed, its tiny body suddenly expanding and transforming into a much larger, powerful version of itself 

 "WAIT, IT CAN DO THAT?!" 

 The transformed slug opened its mouth, unleashing Air Breath, sending enormous spiralling tornadoes sweeping across the surrounding landscape 

 Powerful winds rushed through the Sanctuary of Vaelith, shaking the treetops and scattering leaves across the pathways, while a refreshing breeze swept over Adarlok and Amalia 

 Amalia grabbed her hair as the wind blew it across her face, laughing in disbelief while watching the enormous creature soaring above them 

 "OH MY GOD, THAT LITTLE THING MADE ALL OF THAT?!" 

 Moments later, the slug's attack faded, the tornadoes dispersed and its body shrank back into its original tiny form 

 It descended gently from the sky, landing comfortably upon Adarlok's shoulder as though nothing unusual had happened 

 Amalia stared at the little creature, then at the blaster, before slowly looking back towards Adarlok 

 "Okay... I take back what I said about them looking evil" 

 She paused, watching the slug settle on his shoulder 

 "Actually, no I don't, but that was AMAZING!"
