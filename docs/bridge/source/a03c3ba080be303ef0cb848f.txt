TGC CHAPTER TWO — POWER ANALYSIS AND CONTINUITY NOTES
The Darkness After the Great Sin
Source: user-supplied TGC_The_Grand_Continuum (2).pdf, 20 physical pages.
Credited creator: Draven / Croco / Rico / Diego. Read alongside Chapter One,
not as a replacement for it. Page references below are physical PDF pages.
No Discord message URL for this new PDF was supplied or independently checked.

EVIDENCE KEY
RULE: explicit explanation of a power. FEAT: a narrated outcome in this scene.
INFERENCE: an interpretation supported by the scene, not a new GM-issued rule.
UNKNOWN: information the source does not establish. A character's words can
be informative without proving every boast or joke literally true.

1. A NEW POWER BRANCH: OASIS -> AURA

CH2-01 | Pages 2–3 | RULE / ORIGIN
Arthur prays to Enas for Caesar. White light appears beneath Caesar's skin.
The narrator describes something born through the Oasis Caesar already carries,
yet different from it. Luna explains that through Mana, her lord has awakened
a new energy called Aura, and names Caesar Lord of Aura by Enas's mercy.

Meaning: Aura is a distinct, named energy emerging through an existing Oasis/
Mana foundation in this event. It is not a spelling variant of ROR's Aro, and
the chapter does not describe Vexium, Hexium, a Yellow Stone, or a ROR Class
awakening. The grant is a particular divine intervention, not a recipe any
Oasis holder can repeat merely by praying or touching someone.

The wording also sharpens the older Discord account calling Caesar a maker of
an Oasis replica: the book explicitly credits Enas's intervention and Caesar's
existing Oasis as the initial awakening context. Caesar later distributes the
gift. Do not flatten awakening, origin and later distribution into one action.
Older account: https://discord.com/channels/1535189086258855946/1551573873265213702/1554375916107464777

CH2-02 | Pages 2–3 | FEAT / LIMIT
Arthur dies despite daylight touching him and Caesar's new Aura awakening.
Caesar asks whether it will save Arthur, and it does not do so in the scene.
Chapter One's Oasis of Day strengthens Arthur by day; this is not proof of
immortality, reversal of aging, automatic rescue from death, or universal healing.
Conversely, this one scene does not prove Aura can never heal anyone.

CH2-03 | Page 2 | HISTORICAL FEAT, MECHANISM UNKNOWN
Arthur previously banished Jolt into another realm after Jolt killed innocents.
The chapter supplies the result, not the technique, device, range, cost or
general transport permissions. Do not add realm-banishment to every Aura user.

2. CAESAR — AURA, WIND, DISTRIBUTION AND COMBAT LIMITS

CH2-04 | Page 4 | FEAT
White light gathers on Caesar's arm before an impact breaks the Animalian
King's axe. He eventually defeats the king. Earlier blows hurt and stagger him.
Supported: Aura-assisted physical confrontation and an observed breaking feat.
Not established: invulnerability, all attacks being unblockable, or a numerical
strength/durability tier derived from an unspecified axe.

CH2-05 | Page 4 | FEAT / DISTRIBUTION
Through Caesar's gift, Oasis Aura reaches 300,000 children, with sparks and
glimmers. Pages 6–7 explicitly distinguish Terror's result from the others:
Caesar says none of them did this. The population figure is a distribution
feat, not 300,000 Terror-equivalent beings, a simultaneous casting count,
or proof of identical awakenings in different recipients.

CH2-06 | Page 5 | NAMED FEAT: CAESAR WIND
Caesar Wind throws three bullies backward into a hedge. Page 19 shows it
scattering weapons and disrupting the front rank in the meeting hall.
Supported applications: observed displacement, interruption and space-making.
Unknown: maximum range, cooldown, armor interaction, atmospheric requirements,
whether it always disarms, and whether every later user can inherit it.
Earlier notes call an excluded external gift "Caesar air"; the book's exact
name is Caesar Wind. Record the naming connection as possible, not a silent
equivalence or a new grant to Adelram.

CH2-07 | Pages 6, 10, 12 | FEAT / TRANSFER METHOD
Caesar offers his hand, and the recipient accepts. Terror's transfer is
explicitly Oasis Aura passing between their palms; Venga and Shinichi receive
the same offered-hand sequence. These are voluntary, person-specific grants.
Forced implantation, remote mass transfer, stealing or copying the granting
ability, inheritance by children, and reversibility are not demonstrated.

CH2-08 | Pages 14, 19–20 | FEAT / LIMITS
Shinichi repeatedly defeats Caesar in practice without Aura, identifying a
telegraphed shoulder movement. Caesar later changes his stance and causes an
attacker to miss. During the 400-person ambush he uses waves of white Aura to
clear space, but the gaps refill, he takes injuries, loses breath, nearly
buckles and his Aura flickers. Power and training both matter; enemies exploit
his visible weakening. This scene supports fatigue and pressure on Caesar's
Aura use, not a universal ROR Aro-stamina formula or a 400-person hard cap.

3. TERROR / TOBIAS ALLY — THREE COLORS

CH2-09 | Page 6 | FEAT / TRANSFORMATION
Tobias grows physically to sixteen after receiving Oasis Aura. His left eye
is yellow, right eye blue, and a red third eye opens in his forehead.
This is a particular transformation, not proof that every recipient ages to
sixteen, gains a third eye, or receives the same color expression.
