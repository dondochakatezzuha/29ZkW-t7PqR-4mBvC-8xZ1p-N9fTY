## Text

Awakening: After consuming the Yellow Stone, Abraxas's physical essence, ARO and Oasis began growing beyond infinity without stopping

His flames permanently changed to blue because of the immense physical energy flowing through his body
5 Abilities

Celestial Fist
Abraxas can destroy entire planets with a single punch, while stronger strikes release massive shockwaves capable of damaging nearby celestial bodies


Eternal Blue Flames
His blue flames burn at extraordinary temperatures, destroying physical matter, melting powerful armour and overwhelming ordinary energy defences


Galaxian Authority
Abraxas can create planets, stars and black holes, controlling their gravity and movement to crush enemies or reshape his surroundings


Dimensional Sovereign
He can create dimensions, teleport across vast distances and open portals between universes, allowing him to travel or trap enemies


Mecha Overdrive
His mechanical body can transform its limbs into powerful weapons, fire concentrated energy blasts and greatly amplify his physical attacks
3 Passives

Endless Ascension
His physical strength, physical ARO and Oasis continuously grow beyond infinity without reaching a limit, although this does not make his other abilities unlimited


Galaxian Durability
His body can withstand planet-destroying attacks and survive extreme gravity, radiation and the vacuum of space, although stronger opponents can still injure him


Mechanical Regeneration
His biological and mechanical parts repair themselves using ARO and Oasis, allowing him to recover from severe injuries, although sufficiently powerful attacks can overwhelm his regeneration

DATED v40 RACE FOUNDATION

RACE REGISTER - v40; all five members racial powers combine; no new personal race award

40.1 ADARLOK: MEMBERSHIP, SHARED CONTROL AND SEPARATION

CURRENT USER-CONFIRMED ROSTER: Kaizen + Toby + Loki + Dargan + Adelram. All become one being. All their abilities, eyes, races and other powers combine, and all five can control the single body at once. This is the authoritative current correction for this archive; older three- and four-member descriptions remain dated history.

Toby's merge is completed by Croco. Duplicate time/void functions are described as overlapping, while illusion and reality-to-illusion functionality is explicitly added. Shared access applies to the entire combined kit, including Yang's abilities and, after integration, Lyra's. It is not necessary for each user to personally own the source eye or Stone to direct the merged body's available power.

SEPARATION DEMONSTRATED: Loki exits to reunite with Liana. Kaizen, Adelram, Toby and Dargan remain merged and retain their own powers, but lose Loki's Eye, Stones, gauntlet, sword and everything specifically his. The remaining fusion still uses omnipresence and Shadowics. Loki later re-enters, restoring his kit and the five-member Adarlok. This replaces the older unresolved question of whether separation is possible, but gives no universal duration, cooldown or quantified separation cost.

Both the five-person shared access and the separation boundary matter. No permanent post-separation grant of all extracted powers to Kaizen or Toby is established here. Shift says System owners retain acquired powers after unmerge; mark that as player explanation unless separately ruled. The earlier explicit Yang distribution to three developed Systems remains a dated individual reward record. Lyra's new integration names Adarlok as recipient, without a new individual distribution list.

All constituent racial abilities are part of the merged being, as the user confirms. This is distinct from automatically fusing an external Titan companion into the body or inventing a new race award for each constituent. Keep pre-existing companion contracts and Titan-merge limits unless explicitly changed.

Eye access includes the current eyes of all five: Kaizen's '1'/Void; Toby's Void/Aevor; Loki's Sun/Secret Eye 1/OVRATH; Adelram's '1'/Moon/Secret Eye 2; Dargan's Director/Secret Eye 3/Void. These imply ten distinct current eye types when duplicates are counted once. That is an archive inventory calculation, not a fresh GM anatomical statement or a requirement for ten visible sockets. The scene continues to describe three main eyes. The earlier player sheet's nine-type count omits Toby's Aevor.
