## Text

K proposes fusing five YellowCell flames with Black, White and Grey Flames using Primal. That wording names eight components, but subsequent narration repeatedly calls the completed fusion "seven flames". Preserve this count mismatch without dropping an ingredient. The actual fusion succeeds within the Outside System inventory using Primal and all Stones. It is registered as Judgement Day, rated Dragon x250 million, permanently tied to Loki's Null essence and Primal.

K describes free flame sizing from tiny shapes to dimension-engulfing structures; selected warmth, tickling or burning sensation; burning through all eight Fundamental Manipulations; and precise selection of affected people, objects, energies and structures. Ability suppression builds with exposure near active flames and applies only to weaker opponents, explicitly not stronger ones. No radius, buildup rate or universal bypass of superior authority is specified. Judgement Day cannot be stolen, copied, absorbed or replicated. These are a system description following creation, not a recorded combat test against every type of target.

K gives Dragon x2 billion for Judgement Day combined with all Multiverse Stones and Primal, distinct from the flame's x250 million rating. At Loki's request, K then seals Judgement Day, all Multiverse Stones, excess power and transformations. The strongest accessible form becomes Dragon x500; the original x2 billion form is preserved in storage. The excess signature is suppressed, but safety from attack is not guaranteed. Only Loki's direct authorization releases the seal. No release is completed by this cutoff, so do not present the sealed peak as currently active.

Outside System transfers: permissions and actual recipients

K says eligible powers can go directly into a recipient's Outside System inventory or be implanted into the body/soul of someone without a system. The donor may give the original away or create a separate copy while retaining the original. Recipient consent and capacity to handle the ability are required; an unsuitable transfer can harm them. A permitted transfer is not proof of mastery or a copy of the donor's total power level.

Explicitly permitted: all eight Fundamental Manipulations, White Flames, Black Flames, Lyra's Magic, Jinka's abilities and other powers without exclusive ownership restrictions. Explicitly restricted: Grey Flames, all five of Loki's YellowCell flames, Judgement Day, Primal Staff abilities, Multiverse Stone abilities, the Dragon x2 billion Ultimate Form and its exclusive abilities. Sealed powers remain inaccessible unless Loki authorizes release.

K also explicitly forbids transferring Loki's YellowCell, merging it with another person's YellowCell, or granting it to someone without YellowCell. The stated reasons are Null's possession rules, the system's prohibition on personally bound powers and Loki's unique acquisition. A recipient already having YellowCell or an Outside System does not remove the restriction. This is a ruling on Loki's transfer route; do not infer that all independently acquired YellowCells are impossible.

K sends all-eight-manipulation trade requests to Adelram and Dargen. Dargen accepts and L completes the transfer at 11:04 UTC: all eight are registered and available to learn/control. Track this as Dargen's personal acquisition, separate from earlier access through a merge. The set remains Shadow, Spatial, Energy, Existence, Matter, Reality, Soul and Time; possessing them does not automatically create White/Grey/Judgement Day flames.

Adelram says accept, but X mistakenly presses decline. The source then confirms that the request expired and must be resent. Loki's final recorded action asks K to resend; no new request or successful acceptance has yet been resolved. Do not award Adelram all eight or complete his Energy quest. Loki also asks K to transfer to Kaizen through the no-system route, but no implant or acquisition is resolved. No transfer of White Flames, Black Flames, Lyra or Jinka to a new recipient is shown here.

Adarlok departures and J renamed L

Dargen requests J be renamed L, and the system explicitly confirms the change. L is the same Outside System with a new designation, not a newly acquired system, owner or separate eye. Historical rules referring to J continue to apply to that same system.

At 07:52 UTC Croco narrates Adarlok separating as Dargen and Toby return to individual forms. Dargen then travels independently via Omni; Toby goes through Lust's portal into Loki's Shadow Realm. These are completed departures, not just requests. Their subsequent quests and family travel are personal scenes, with no resolved re-entry.
