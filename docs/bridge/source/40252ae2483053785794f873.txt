Adelram: Imperial Horizon and the Forgotten Sovereign
COMPLETED TRAINING AND TRANSFORMATION; CONFLICTING NUMERICAL STATEMENTS RETAINED

Shift leaves for his own realm and sends his son Albion to continue Adelram's Time training. Albion communicates through signs translated by X; the source does not establish deafness or blindness. Ignis remains initially and later departs. Ordinary Imperial Vision is a temporal mirage left as the original body advances in time, not a physical clone. Albion can pass through it and disrupt its temporal stability.

Holding the Time Branch stabilizes the mirage against two disturbances. Without holding it, Adelram initially fails after several seconds. By directing time through concentration instead of forcing more energy, he succeeds, then maintains the technique through three disturbances for another ten seconds before losing concentration. These are observed training results, not an unlimited baseline duration.

At 10:20 UTC an unidentified voice announces Aro Zone Imperial Horizon Form. Adelram becomes an enormous primordial-looking creature with four arms, seven horns, obsidian armour and a temporal heart. The explicit awakening panel gives Dragon x450 million and 2.5 trillion temporal mirages. These special mirages physically affect their surroundings while ordinary external forces cannot affect them; sufficiently authoritative beings such as Albion and Ignis can. Albion later dissolves one with a finger, demonstrating that exception. This appearance is a transformation, not a newly awarded biological race or numbered eye.

Adelram attributes the voice to Oak. Albion/X identify Oak as the Time Branch's consciousness. The later control scene narrates 2.3 trillion independently acting mirages and names the ancient temporal awakening The Forgotten Sovereign. That same scene has Ignis say Dragon x750 million, conflicting with the x450 million awakening panel. Both figures, and the 2.5 versus 2.3 trillion counts, remain flagged; no explicit correction explains them. Do not silently replace one with the other or combine them.

The form ends and all mirages disappear. X states a maximum duration of 25 minutes; no cooldown is given. Albion then finishes the lesson and leaves. The Eternal Sovereign quest offers unlimited duration only after learning Energy Manipulation, mastering it, and applying it to the transformation (0/3). X admits not knowing how to teach it and corrects her mistaken claim that Adelram already has energy-based abilities. At this cutoff, the promised unlimited duration is unearned and the trade for all eight manipulations has been declined by X; a resend is requested but not completed.
