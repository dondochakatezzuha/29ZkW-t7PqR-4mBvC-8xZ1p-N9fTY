TITAN REGISTER - v38; six new Jerial weapon assignments and merge limits

38.7 PLAYER MERGE, TITAN MERGE AND INHERITED COSTS

Croco explicitly permits players to merge. Adding players combines their buffs and nerfs, and he says all eight or nine current players could become one. This is a system permission, not an already completed fusion or a promise that every combination is optimal. A high ARO drain or other limit is carried into the merged result along with the strengths. No fixed multiplier, duration, cost-splitting formula, control hierarchy or immunity to incompatible effects is specified.

Titan merge is different. Croco rejects the proposed sequence of merging with a Titan and then using player merge. The two systems cannot be treated as a freely stacking route to obtain all Titan and player benefits at once. Existing Titan contracts remain individual unless an actual scene establishes another result.

For team planning, list every contributor's useful functions and costs before proposing a merge: information and connection control, energy supply, spatial routing, defenses, summons, sustained damage and ARO consumption all matter. Player mockery or the ranking alone is not evidence that a lower-ranked teammate adds nothing. No named team fusion is completed in the new messages.

38.9 JERIAL WEAPONS FOR SIX TITAN CONTRACT HOLDERS

Croco assigns Jerial weapons to the six existing Titan contract holders. The attached designs were visually inspected; they do not contain a move list. These are not all swords, and design colors alone do not establish elements, ranges or damage.

Toby — Mother Prana: blue/purple double-ended bladed polearm.
Arlo — Dromak: paired curved swords, one red/black and one white/gold.
Adelram — Sylvara: paired pale/gold plantlike or vine/wood scythes.
Dargan — Kryvorn: blue crystalline curved scythe/hook-shaped weapon.
Loki — Varkhazar: large jagged gold/red/black blade.
Kaizen — Vaelthoryn: gold/white bow, with quiver and arrows.
