Abyss Water doesn't completely fill someone's Hexium with Vexium or bring them to the point they would have naturally reached; it only pushes the Vexium inside their Hexium to around the middle. Think of a person's Hexium like a huge empty tube that slowly fills with Vexium throughout their life. Every day they're adding small buckets through things like fighting, gaining experience, developing their personality, maturing, and simply living. Eventually, someone who keeps doing this will completely fill their Hexium and naturally awaken with all the Vexium they were meant to have. During awakening, a massive amount of ARO is released inside the Hexium and combines with the Vexium, creating Aether. Abyss Water is basically the shortcut: instead of patiently using buckets, someone grabs a hose and rapidly fills the Hexium, but once it reaches the halfway point, the hose shuts off and forces an early awakening. That's why Abyss Water is useful but not amazing. It gives someone enough Vexium to awaken and become an average, capable user without spending all that time naturally developing themselves, but it doesn't give them the foundation or potential of someone whose Vexium was allowed to fully mature. You're basically choosing to awaken sooner and be average instead of waiting and potentially awakening much stronger
