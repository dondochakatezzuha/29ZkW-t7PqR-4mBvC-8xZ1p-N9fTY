CH2-11 | Pages 6–7 | FEAT / CONTROL LIMIT
Terror suspends water and repairs the barrel. Later he lifts more river water
than intended, carrying bags downstream and leaving fish suspended. Strong
output does not equal precise control. The scene supports practice and a clear
target volume; it does not give a measured maximum lifting capacity.

CH2-12 | Page 9 | FEAT / DESTRUCTIVE EFFECT
He says "Terror"; red, yellow and blue cross the sky. The threatening robber
vanishes with no body or shadow left on the road. Record this as the observed
disappearance/destruction result. The source does not establish soul erasure,
erasure from all timelines, inability to be revived, or automatic success
against every god. It does not explicitly specify whether the spoken name is
a mandatory activation condition in all uses.

CH2-13 | Pages 13, 16 | FEATS / PRACTICAL CREATION
Yellow light repairs the boat's split boards. Later Terror rebuilds a doorway
in yellow light after a fire. Creation is demonstrated as repair and shelter,
not only offense. A reader can infer a useful construction role, but object
persistence, material identity, cost, complexity and living-tissue creation
are not defined here. Do not import Kaizen's YellowCell material rules into it.

CH2-14 | Page 14 | FEAT / STARTLE RESPONSE
A dropped plate surprises Terror; his yellow eye flares before he turns, and a
stone ridge breaks through the floor. No one is hurt, but the scene shows an
unintended environmental effect. This is not a guarantee of a perfect automatic
shield or an identical response to every threat. Fear remains after awakening.

CH2-15 | Pages 17, 20 | DIFFERENT EVIDENCE TYPES
"I can be extremely small and quiet" is followed by a joke about being
emotionally quiet. It does not establish size-changing. During the rescue,
everyone in the hall hears "Terror" inside their ears while the sky takes on
the three colors. That is a narrated shared auditory manifestation, not proof
of unrestricted mind control, mind-reading or forced obedience.

4. VENGA / DARIUS VENGA ZION — PURPLE FORCE

CH2-16 | Pages 9–10 | IDENTITY / AWAKENING FEAT
Darius Venga Zion, called Venga, is the brother protecting three sisters.
After accepting Caesar's hand, purple light reaches the orchard wall, fallen
apples rise slightly and force through the handshake shakes cups indoors.
The book shows an awakening and external force; it does not name a ROR Class.

CH2-17 | Page 13 | FEAT
Venga steadies the damaged boat with a shivering purple glow while Terror
repairs it and Shinichi rows. This is a direct example of complementary work.
The exact mechanic—gravity, telekinesis, pressure, or a broader Aura technique—
is not specified by this chapter, so describe the observed stabilization.

CH2-18 | Page 20 | GROUP SCENE, ATTRIBUTION LIMITED
Purple radiance appears below sealed doors, the hinges strain and doors burst
open. Venga is then shown wrapped in purple light. The association strongly
suggests his involvement, but the prose does not individually label every
effect's caster. Record the group rescue and the likely association without
inventing a named door-breaking move, unlimited barrier bypass, or a new stat.

The chapter does not itself name "Hue" or establish PurpleCell. Existing

5. SHINICHI — SKILL, WHITE GHOST AND THE RESCUE IMAGE

CH2-19 | Pages 11–12 | FEAT / SELF-REPORT
The group encounters forty defeated armed men and Shinichi with injured
knuckles/arm. He says he has no ability and is sixteen. He accepts Aura only
later. This is evidence of exceptional pre-grant skill and of being injured;
his statement is not a measurement proving no underlying Mana or soul exists.
It does not give his strength, speed or durability a numerical tier.

CH2-20 | Page 12 | FEAT / REPUTATION
After receiving Aura, his footsteps become harder to hear and pale eyes appear
through night mist. Travelers call him the White Ghost. Supported: quieter
movement and a distinct presentation. Unknown: invisibility, intangibility,
teleportation, zero sound in all circumstances, and whether he creates all mist.

CH2-21 | Pages 14–15 | FEAT / TRAINING
He teaches Caesar and Terror to reduce obvious movement tells. He later catches
a falling bowl without looking. These support awareness and coordination; a
single catch does not prove omniscience or a new passive sensory power.

CH2-22 | Page 20 | GROUP SCENE, MECHANISM UNKNOWN
Fog enters and a large wolf-like shape with pale eyes emerges as the Legion
arrives. The prose suggests a White Ghost connection but does not explicitly
say Shinichi transformed, summoned a wolf, projected an illusion, or possessed
it. Save the imagery as an observed manifestation. Do not choose a mechanism
and then grant all associated abilities.

6. NEMESIS, ARTHUR AND THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN POWER AND POSITION

CH2-23 | Pages 17–20 | FEATS / NON-MECHANICAL ADVANTAGES
Nemesis has purple skin, a dark presence and a legion. He arranges the meeting
and surrounds Caesar with 400 armed people, including elevated ranged pressure
and ground-level attackers. Caesar declines an early opening while trying to
reach his brother. Nemesis's command and ambush are shown; this chapter does
not provide his supernatural move list or identify his color as PurpleCell.
