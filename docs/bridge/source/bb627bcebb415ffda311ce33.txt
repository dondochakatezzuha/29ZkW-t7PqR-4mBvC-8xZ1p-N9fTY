41.16 ARLO, DROMAK, SLUGS AND OTHER CAST UPDATES

Arlo's current training develops Event Break interference with telekinesis, Orbit directional attention, liquid-gravity defense, VAST precision and World Drop. Dromak's huge physical essence prevents an immediate oversized Titan Form and requires shrinking. A one-to-one training exchange and successful rock exercises are not an awarded full-power win or Galaxian King acquisition. Dromak remains Arlo's Titan, outside the latest five-person Adarlok roster.

Kaizen's Common Air Slug is reaffirmed in his channel. Toby is awarded a Common Electric Slug; a 0.5-percent dark chance is mentioned but does not by itself prove a dark outcome. Dargen is explicitly awarded a dark Common Healing Slug. A separate Common Fire result replies to the display name Dargan; because the surrounding roll conversation has identity ambiguity, keep that record separately rather than silently replacing the explicit Healing result. The handbook's earlier Toby Common Fire entry is historical beside the newer Electric result.

Monarch's previously supplied Yellow Stone Space/Mystia techniques remain: False Arrival, Mind Swap, Puppet Step, Ambush and Absolute Checkmate, with their original ranges, durations and resistance limits. No newly completed Monarch awakening, eye replacement or race award appears here. The current sourcebook and registers retain all named characters, races, OASIS, Null, ARO, Cells, Jera, Stones, Titans and devices across the whole saved server.

Orion's car-impact durability and Adelram's repair/memory-removal scene are documented demonstrations, not a general new family power inheritance rule. Amelia is a sought Galaxian royal; no acquisition from meeting her has occurred by the cutoff.
