9. Damien, Azreal, Maeve and Athena
OBSERVED TECHNIQUES, BLOODLINE EXPLANATION AND TRAINING

Azreal wins the first physical round against Damien. Damien subsequently shows blue eyes during improved blocking/strategy; no new numbered eye or Cell is identified. Staff transformations/clones, levitation and feints occur, while Azreal uses shadow intangibility. K estimates Damien's IQ as 2665 and Azreal's as 3372: these are in-fiction system estimates, not real psychological measurements. A staff collision explodes across the arena; both boys remain conscious with minor injuries. Varkhazar emerges from Loki's skin to shield Maeve and Athena.

Azreal's green eyes activate an automatic defence against Loki's attacks. K attributes it to Aviala's bloodline as former Queen of the North; the exact ability name remains unknown. This is not an award of GreenCell, Time Stone or an identified TGC eye. Tests show attacks fast enough to exceed his reactions can bypass it, and Loki stops before impact. The eyes return to normal. Deliberate activation and complete invulnerability are not established.

Maeve first struggles with stance, punches and teleportation and becomes discouraged. After Loki asks K to train her, the 10:41 scene shows an effective combination: teleport behind Loki, staff strike, Shadow Form through his body, punch, then swap positions with her staff and kick while K controls Loki's body. K says she outsmarted him using techniques learned minutes earlier. This is successful training and a specific landed combination, not proof she is stronger than every character. The actual result supersedes her initial inability. Her staff, teleportation, Shadow Form and position-swap belong in her own entry.

Athena remains a five-year-old child participating in the family scenes and cheering on Maeve. No full premium manipulation set or new combat feat is awarded to her here. Loki's claim that his children are strongest in the entire realm is a player's boast. General jokes about numerous wives/children or universal Null adulthood rules are not a canon census or a proven race mechanic.
