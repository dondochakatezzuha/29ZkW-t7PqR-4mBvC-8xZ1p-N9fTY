Loki family, Toby and the Shadow Realm

Liana wants another child eventually but chooses time together first. Separately, Aviala consents to K accelerating a pregnancy: Time Manipulation advances nine months and Existence Manipulation removes pain/complications. A healthy child is born. This is a new birth, not a retcon reviving the previously lost unborn child, and Liana has no new completed pregnancy here.

At Aviala's request the child is physically aged to five. K uses a Soul Manipulation connection and described Mind Manipulation to impart selected memories without overwhelming him, then Existence Manipulation grants reduced Shadow Manipulation and Black Flames. Those powers are weaker than Loki's and constrained by the child's own energy. Do not award the child Primal, all Stones, Grey Flames, Judgement Day or the father's full rank. The narration calls the child a son; Loki later asks the name and sex, but no answer or personal name is recorded by the cutoff. This use of Mind does not revise the enumerated set of eight Fundamental Manipulations.

Lust retrieves Toby from Adarlok and transports him through a portal into Loki's Shadow Realm. Loki uses Primal to amplify existing Shadow Manipulation, constructing and maintaining a black castle for Toby. This grants a place to stay, not ownership of Primal or all Shadow Manipulation. Toby later requests to leave and look for Artemis and Kaja; that reunion is not resolved. The All Father fight and Toby's promise remain pending.

The players settle their sin labels as Loki/Pride, Arlo/Envy, Toby/Sloth and Adelram/Gluttony. An invitation for Kaizen to replace Wrath is accepted conversationally but no transfer of Wrath's powers or completed arrival is narrated. Loki's plans for Apateon's golden flames and Lod Yang remain goals, not victories or acquisitions. Shadow Realm access is stated to require Loki's permission; owning spatial movement alone does not establish unrestricted entry.

48.10 Paja, Soren, Laja and the wider family record

Kaizen meets Paja, Soren and Laja while physically unmerged. Paja acknowledges conflict over Kharic traditions and her own mistakes while insisting on accountability for Kaizen's absence and threats. Through Aeth, Paja and Soren see his memories and the Caelen battle. Their harsh appraisals, praise of Loki and claims about past events are character judgments, not an objective power ranking or a reliable replacement for directly narrated history. Paja initially denies Loki killed his wives even though separate scenes establish their deaths and revival; retain her limited perspective.

Kaizen says he will stay. Paja and Soren acknowledge that choice, Laja welcomes him, and the scene resolves a tentative return home with trust to rebuild. It is not instant forgiveness or a fully repaired marriage. Kaizen's later Null training happens during this visit. Paja explains that Laja's Kharic heritage slows her physical development despite the elapsed years. This is an attributed race-related explanation for Laja, not a quantified aging law for every Kharic or a new race acquisition.

Adelram returns to Vaja, Lucia and baby Orion. Later narration places Orion with Shift and Ignis playing with miniature planets outside the castle without explaining how he moved from Vaja's arms; record the continuity gap, not an invented teleport ability for Orion. Paja's retrospective accusation about Orion does not establish a new present death: the contemporary family scenes show him alive. No new power grant for Vaja, Lucia, Orion, Paja or Soren occurs here.

No new all-player strength ranking, permanent combined multiplier or resolved Arlo re-entry is established in this interval. Mariam, Toby, Abraxas and Max retain their prior sourced foundations, subject to the actual merge roster. Existing Tyreror water-from-air requires Yellowstone; the retained Mercury Null and prior Cell composition rules are unchanged. Preserve those foundations alongside all newly sourced character updates, without treating Kaizen as the only character or protagonist.
