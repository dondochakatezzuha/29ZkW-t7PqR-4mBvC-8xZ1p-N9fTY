1. Manipulation transfers and the nine tails

The eight Fundamental Manipulations previously transferred by Loki to Dargen, Adelram and Kaizen remain Shadow, Spatial, Energy, Existence, Matter, Reality, Soul and Time. Receipt does not confer mastery: the new gifts start at level 0. Kaizen's pre-existing Matter practice is not erased by this rule. Arlo asks for all eight at the end of this update, but no completed transfer to him is shown. Toby likewise asks for power without a resolved award.

Draven initially says the tails do not possess Kaizen's gifted manipulations because they were given to him rather than developed by him. Croco's later 08:37 UTC narration makes a narrower distinction: connected tails CAN access Kaizen's manipulations through his power, but do not each independently own them. Giving each of nine tails eight independent manipulations would require separate transfers; the proposed total of 72 is not an accomplished grant. Preserve the earlier denial as a dated disagreement, then apply the later explicit borrowed-access explanation.

The forwarded nine-tail description assigns Raiko lightning acceleration/interception, Zeph wind and atmospheric control, Kairo physical enhancement, Veya barriers/absorption, Ryn recovery/vitality, Draz gravity, Sora sensing/analysis, Zarek short-range spatial movement and Aeth consciousness/coordination. This is a repost of an earlier description, not nine new unlocks. Their distinct Oasis signatures remain linked to Kaizen. Isolated Oasis stays stable but does not develop a consciousness in the experiment; all nine tails remain safe.

The 08:42 experiment fails: Energy Manipulation does not activate Null Heart, Matter responds but the attempted Shadow/Energy combination fails, and the proposed three-to-five-to-eight fusion cannot begin with untrained abilities. Caesar is not revived. Borrowing an untrained manipulation does not make the tail a master of it. Loki explicitly declines to transfer White Flames because of complications and suggests a blueprint instead. Eclipsecell/mega-flame combinations, independent tail gifts and a revival technique remain proposals, not new Cells, flame forms or completed team methods.
