Dargen: Oasis Zone, speed tiers and upper-god selection
COMPLETED ZONE ENTRY; FUTURE REWARDS AND SELECTION REMAIN UNRESOLVED

After attempting Oasis Zone, Dargen sees Rin and Kinji beside twisting lightning and golden light. L announces entry into Oasis Zone. Opening his eyes reveals an empty mental realm with the God of Speed. L explicitly says it is not Dargen's physical location and does not know the being's capabilities. The God of Speed says Dargen has finally arrived and asks him closer. Dargen asks to walk toward him, but no reward, new speed tier or resolved next encounter follows by the cutoff.

This is a successful Oasis Zone entry, distinct from Croco's earlier exclusion of Dargen's Lightning/Aure/Rinkinji from Aro Zone. Do not retcon that exclusion or award Oasis Zone to every character. Neither Rin/Kinji's appearance nor the encounter provides a new biological race, numerical speed multiplier or universal rule for the zone.

Earlier Croco calls Omniversal Supremacy the final TGC level of speed, used by beings such as Shift. His description says ordinary Omnipresence-level movement would look like human running to that user, with other action effectively frozen by comparison. This is a setting explanation, not real-world physics or a new feat by Dargen. Croco's "5% fast" remark lacks a defined denominator and must not become an exact measured fraction of Shift's speed.

Croco says attaining or comprehending that level requires becoming an upper god, being chosen by the Councils and passing their test. It is not a spin reward or a guaranteed training route. He describes death in the test as eternal within the game, with loss of continued play on the main account. He estimates Loki alone at 0.1% selection chance and the other current players at 0%, citing unusual flames, saving the multiverse and Council recognition. These are current attributed estimates, not a new appointment, guaranteed future probability or immutable player ranking. Player requests for 99%, irrelevant/independence speed labels and comparisons do not grant them.
