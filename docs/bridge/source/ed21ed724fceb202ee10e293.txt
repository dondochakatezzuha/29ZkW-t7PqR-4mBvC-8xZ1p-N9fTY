45.1 SOVEREIGN FRACTURE: SOURCE-CONFIRMED REPLACEMENT

Croco's 09:41 statement identifies Adelram's old ability two as redundant with the System; the 09:45 replacement now corroborates the user correction already delivered in v44.1. Sovereign Fracture replaces the old power-gaining ability, not the eye's System function. TGC/Secret Eye 2 remains Adelram's. No other numbered ability is changed by this replacement.

Sovereign Fracture [Controllable]
,

The wielder creates a tiny, invisible fracture within an opponent's Fire, Shadow, or physical abilities, forming a hidden opening that can be exploited to bypass their defences. The fracture cannot be perceived by the opponent, even if they possess a higher power level than the wielder. It cannot affect nonphysical abilities such as Time, Reality, Soul, or Existence Manipulation

The concealment explicitly applies even against a stronger opponent. That is a perception rule; the opening still has to be exploited and the text does not promise automatic damage or victory. Fire, Shadow and physical abilities are in scope. Nonphysical Time, Reality, Soul and Existence Manipulation are explicitly excluded. Range, duration, cost and cooldown are unstated.

Croco also clarifies the acquisition history: Loki deliberately spun for Eye 1; Dargen obtained Eye 3 through luck rather than selecting it; Adelram spun for Eye 2 when no other eye remained. These comments do not award extra copies. Adelram is outside current Adarlok; the replacement does not automatically transfer to it.

45.3 TRANSFORMATION STACKING AND RESTORED SYSTEM ACCESS

Resolved progression: Null Transformation plus Black Flames operate independently in the same body; Abraxas's form is added with increased strain; Max's transformation is added with much greater available energy and further physical pressure. Aeth coordinates while Caesar stabilizes the Null Heart and Veya prepares safeguards. Initial compatibility is demonstrated, not effortless indefinite maintenance.

Croco later includes Toby alongside those forms and integrates the acquired Replar, Jinka and Lyra abilities. The entry scene calls this five transformations plus integrated acquired abilities. No independent resurrected Replar/Jinka/Lyra people are thereby added. The corrected Jinka dialogue confirms that distinction.

Real Dargen's resolved return then adds his transformation alongside Max, Abraxas, Toby, Black Flames and Null. J and Outside System access return through Dargen's actual eye. His real participation is distinct from the previously retained Platonic replica. This advances v44's five-real-member state to six real members; no resolved departure by Arlo or Toby is shown merely because they join a faction or leave chat.

J explicitly warns that merging individuals does not automatically add their full individual multipliers. Compatibility and actual contribution need evaluation. No formula multiplying every form, every tail and every offensive multiplier is established. The final invitations to Mariam and others remain pending, so do not add Mariam's Genesis or transformation to the current active assembly.
