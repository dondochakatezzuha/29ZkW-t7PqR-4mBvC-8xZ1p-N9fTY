# HOW TO PLAY / GAME GUIDE

Welcome to the game! This is an **action-based RPG focused on character-driven storytelling**, similar to a simplified D&D-style experience.

You do not need to be an expert writer. Just create a character, interact with the world, make choices, and react to what happens around you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR CHARACTER

Go to **<#1535189087760154671>** and create your character.

Use this template:

```yaml
Age:
Sex:
Height:
Weight:
Description:
Wears:
```

You do not need an extremely detailed character. Even a simple character can develop naturally throughout the story.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# HOW CHARACTER INTERACTIONS WORK

You control **your character**, including their actions, dialogue, and decisions.

### Dialogue

```text
Civic: "I'm not running from this."
```

### Actions

```text
Civic steps forward and raises his weapon.
```

### Combined Example

```text
Civic narrows his eyes, gripping his weapon tighter. "If you want a fight, you've got one."
```

## Do Not Control Other Players' Characters

### Bad Example

```text
Civic punches Arlo and knocks him unconscious.
```

### Better Example

```text
Civic throws a punch toward John.
```

Let **John decide how his character reacts**, whether they dodge, block, or get hit.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# ACTION AND COMBAT

Combat works through character interactions and your character's abilities.

Describe what your character **tries to do** instead of automatically deciding the outcome.

### Example

```text
Civic rushes toward the enemy and swings his sword toward their side.
```

The other player, Game Master, or staff can determine how the situation continues.

Be creative. Your character is not limited to repeating the same attack over and over.

# CHARACTER DEATH

Character death is a permanent part of the game.

If your character dies, you must create a new character to continue playing. Your new character will begin their own story and will not inherit the previous character's progress, abilities, relationships, or achievements.

Treat death seriously, make meaningful choices, and remember that every character's story can come to an end.

# ARO & CLASSES

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# WHAT IS ARO?

**Aro is the conscious life essence that flows through every living being.**

Your Aro is not just a source of power. It has its own consciousness and can make decisions based on your character's true nature.

You **guide your Aro**, but you do not completely control it.

Your choices, experiences, personality, and connection with your Aro can affect how it develops.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# THE 9 ARO CLASSES

Once your Aro awakens, you will be randomly assigned **1 of 9 Classes**.

You cannot choose or inherit your Class.

Your Aro decides your path.

### Aure

The power to control the essence of **light** itself.

### Tyreror

The power to control all **liquids**, including water and even blood.

### Infernia

The power to control **flames**.

White flames are extremely difficult to obtain and control.

### Gravitos

The power to control **gravity** itself.

### Astria

The power to manipulate **space**.

### Mystia

The power connected to controlling and manipulating **one's mind**.

### Tempest

The power to control **lightning**.

### Class 8

**CLASSIFIED**

### Class 9

**CLASSIFIED**

> **Every Class has the potential to become powerful.**

Your strength depends on how your Aro develops, how you train, your choices, and how you use your abilities.

Two people with the same Class may become completely different in strength and abilities.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# ARO GROWTH

Your Aro constantly reacts to who your character becomes.

## Your Choices Matter

Your actions can cause your Aro to:

* Grow stronger
* Become weaker
* Develop differently from others with the same Class

Your Aro reflects **who you truly are**, not just what Class you possess.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Green** — Your Aro is growing stronger.

**Blue** — Your Aro is close to awakening.

**Yellow** — Your Aro is currently evolving.

> Once your Aro awakens, the **Blue glow can never appear again**.

# ARO DEVELOPMENT

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# CONNECTING WITH YOUR ARO

Before awakening, your character can communicate with their Aro and build a connection with it.

The stronger and more genuine that connection is, the more potential your character may unlock.

However, your Aro knows your character's **true intentions**.

Trying to manipulate or connect with your Aro purely for power may actually weaken it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# WHAT DOES MY ARO LOOK LIKE?

Your Aro can take a form that your character wants it to have.

It could appear as:

* An animal
* A creature
* A humanoid
* A strange spirit
* A monster
* Objects
* Buildings
* Something completely unique

However, the form **cannot be NSFW**.

Remember: even if your Aro takes a form you choose, it is still a **conscious life essence** with its own thoughts and decisions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# ARO EVOLUTION

As your character and Aro continue to grow, they may eventually reach a **Critical Evolution Point**.

A rare **Yellow Evolution Stone** can completely rewrite an Aro.

This may create:

* A completely new Class
* New abilities
* New forms
* Powers that have never existed before

These Evolutions are shaped by your character's:

* Personality
* Experiences
* Decisions
* True nature

Because of this, no two Evolutions are guaranteed to be the same.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# WHEN CAN I AWAKEN OR EVOLVE?

There is no exact level where awakening or evolution must happen.

It can happen:

* Early in the story
* During a major event
* After significant character development
* Much later in the storyline
* On the verge of death

Your character's actions and the direction of the story can influence when these moments occur.

# STORY & WORLD LORE

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# THE STORY — GAME 1

Game 1 took place on **Aethelgard (Nova-7)** in the **RoR verse**.

The main threat was **Vespa**, a powerful and highly intelligent man seeking to overcome the limitations of Aro.

His goal was to gain access to power beyond those limitations and eventually reach a form known as **"The Final."**

Players were recruited by the **SS**, whose main mission was to stop Vespa before he could obtain this power.

However, the world contained far more than just the main storyline.

Throughout Game 1, characters could explore, form relationships, fight enemies, train, uncover secrets, and discover hidden missions as the story developed.

> **SPOILER ALERT:** The SS was completely destroyed, and almost all players died.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# THE ARO TITANS

Long before the current events, the first being connected to Aro was **Mother Prana**.

She is known as the original **Aro Titan** and the first being to possess Aro.

There are **six Titans in total**, each connected to protecting the world.

They are not gods. They are essentially **nature's final defense system**.

The Titans normally remain dormant and only awaken if they believe their world is facing destruction.

# THE ETERNAL

If all six Titans gather together, they can unleash **The Eternal**.

This event creates a powerful blast that reaches into the sky, combining the power of all six Titans to create a **Seventh Titan**.

The Seventh Titan can trigger a cosmic reset similar to a new Big Bang, destroying the old universe and beginning the cycle again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE

**Your character is yours.**

Make choices.

Develop relationships.

Make enemies.

Train.

Fail.

Change.

Your character does not need to become the strongest person.

The fun comes from watching them **grow throughout the story**.

All you require is a brain.

Just ask yourself:

> **"What would my character do?"**

Then write it and see where the story takes you.
