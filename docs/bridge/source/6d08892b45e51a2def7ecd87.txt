Kaizen achieves a stable fusion and tests the proposed Shadowcell Flames

The previously stable but incompletely fused mixture now becomes a genuine fusion. At 11:11 UTC Aeth repeatedly expands, compresses and rearranges the isolated sphere while Kairo maintains compression. Sora identifies excessive Shadow and instructs a reduction. After several failed attempts, Black Flames, Shadow and YellowCell contribute equally, forming a stable black-and-gold flame. The narration announces NEW ENERGY FUSION DISCOVERED, STATUS STABLE. It remains isolated from Kaizen's body.

At 11:13 UTC Kaizen tests a small quantity against a distant empty lunar crater. Veya establishes protective barriers and Sora reports no living organisms in the target area. The impact turns the crater black, creates golden cracks, explodes and collapses inward. The crater disappears, leaving a deep circular hole. The tail explanation assigns material destruction to Black Flames, confinement of the destruction to Shadow, and maintenance of the fusion to YellowCell. The source marks the fusion SUCCESSFULLY TESTED. This establishes a concentrated destructive test, not universal erasure, unlimited energy or a numerical rank.

At 11:15 UTC Kaizen asks to name it "shadowcell flames". Save Shadowcell Flames as his proposed working name; no subsequent naming confirmation or formal ability registration is shown before the GM pauses the game. Aeth says further development could make it an entirely new ability. No costs, cooldown, maximum size, combat performance or completed internal integration are specified. This is a fusion using YellowCell, not a newly identified natural Cell lineage or a replacement for YellowCell.

Aeth also contacts Paja aboard the returning spaceship and relays that Kaizen will return after training. Paja and Laja respond. This confirms communication during that journey but does not prove unlimited range or completed arrival home. Kaizen's later complaint that he did not awaken Aro Zone does not erase earlier entry; no restored Null access or new Aro Zone transformation is resolved in this interval.
