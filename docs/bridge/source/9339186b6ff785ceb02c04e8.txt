# @everyone  ARO

Aro is the life essence that flows through every living being. Once your Aro awakens, you're randomly assigned 1 of 9 unique Classes. You can't choose your Class or inherit it it's the path your Aro decides for you.

And yes. Aro is a concious life essence it decides everything FOR you but you guide it to.

Each Class grants its own unique abilities, from 🌊 Tyrerors, who command liquids, to 🔥 Infernias, who wield flames, 🌌 Gravitos, who bend gravity, and many more. Every Class has the potential to become just as powerful through training and mastery.

As your Aro grows, so does your Class, unlocking stronger abilities and new techniques. But for the few who reach a Critical Evolution Point, a legendary 💛 Yellow Evolution Stone may completely rewrite their Aro, creating a Class that has never existed before. These Evolutions are shaped by the user's personality, experiences, and true nature, making every one completely unique.

Feature 1: Aro Growth

• Every choice you make affects your Aro, causing it to either grow stronger or weaker over time

• Two players with the same Class can awaken with completely different levels of Aro depending on the choices they've made

• Your Aro reflects the person you've become, not just the Class you have

Feature 2: Aro Colours

• Before awakening, you can use !glow to check the current state of your Aro

• Red means your Aro is weakening

• Green means your Aro is growing stronger

• Blue means your Aro is close to awakening

• Yellow means your Aro is currently evolving

• Once your Aro awakens, the Blue glow can never appear again

Feature 3: Connecting with Aro

• Before awakening, you can communicate with your Aro and build a connection with it

• The stronger and more genuine your connection, the stronger your future Class will become

• Aro always knows your true intentions, so trying to connect with it only for power will weaken your Aro instead
