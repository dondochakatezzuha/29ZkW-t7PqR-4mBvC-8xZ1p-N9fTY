MODE

MODE lets you 1v1 other players using characters you have unlocked.

When MODE first releases, you can use your own character or other players' characters you've unlocked. More characters and companions will be added later.

HOW TO PLAY
@GM and ask to play MODE.
GM will show you the characters you have unlocked. Everyone will have different characters.

Example: Mason might have Vespa unlocked while Kaizen might have Kaijen.

Pick your character and find someone to 1v1.
Both players will be sent to a large fighting field. A referee will count down from 5.
GM spins a wheel to decide who gets the first move.

There are no rounds. The fight ends when someone dies or gives up.

FIGHTING

When it's your turn, explain what your character tries to do.

GOOD:

I run towards him, jump to the left and use Control.

BAD:

I run towards him, dodge all his attacks and punch him.

You cannot dodge while you're the one attacking. You can move or position yourself in a way that might help you avoid the opponent's next attack.

When someone attacks you, you can't just say:

I dodge.

You have to explain how you try to avoid it or counter it using your character.

You also can't decide that your attack automatically hits.

BAD:

I punch him in the jaw and break it.

GOOD:

I attempt to punch him in the jaw.

You control what your character tries to do, not what happens to the other player.

The moves above are examples only. You cannot copy them during an actual fight.

# FIGHTING NPCS

NPC fights have 4 difficulties:

**Easy**
Weaker characters like Jasmine.

**Normal**
Stronger characters who know how to fight.

**Hard**
Powerful characters where mistakes can cost you the fight.

**Extremely Difficult**
The strongest characters, like Vespa. These fights are meant to be extremely hard to win.

# FIGHTING PLAYERS

Player fights have no difficulty.

**The player IS the difficulty.**

A strong character doesn't guarantee a win. How well you use them matters more.

# UNLOCKING CHARACTERS

When MODE launches, you'll have access to **your characters, other players' characters and companions.**

More characters must be earned by playing.

Complete quests, challenges, fights and unlock new universes to grow your roster.

The stronger and rarer the character, the harder they'll be to unlock.
