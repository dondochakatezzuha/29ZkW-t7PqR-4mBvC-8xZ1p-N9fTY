## Text

Caesar leaves the meeting location and a return deadline; the team insists on
an earlier rescue threshold. They arrive while he is injured. A credible team
plan has an extraction condition, not an assumption that its strongest member
cannot be overwhelmed. This is strategic interpretation from the story, not
a supernatural ability or an assertion that rescue can never arrive too late.

7. WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE MASTER

- Add Aura as a distinct documented TWN-side power with its origin and transfer
  evidence. Keep Aro, Aura, OASIS and Null separately searchable.
- Add Caesar, Terror, Venga and Shinichi historical power dossiers; do not give
  their complete kits to current players just because souls/traits are related.
- Add actual support feats—repairs, stabilization, transport preparation and
  coordinated rescue—alongside battle feats.
- Keep gift provenance: Caesar's divine awakening and distribution do not make
  externally obtained Caesar Wind, Venga Hue or Ignis's Shadow Level automatic
  Yellow Stone inheritance.
- Treat colors as system-specific. Terror's yellow creation is not established
  YellowCell; Venga's purple force is not established PurpleCell; his or
  Shinichi's effects do not create a new ROR Class without a GM statement.
- Preserve the prior Chapter One distinction: the book gives Kael Arthur/Moon,
  blue from birth; older conflicting player lore remains historical evidence.

8. NEW BOOK CONTINUITY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

After the Great Sin, Arthur dies with Caesar beside him. Enas awakens Aura
through Caesar's Oasis; Caesar defeats Animalia's tyrant and spreads the gift.
He recruits Terror, Venga and Shinichi, and Caesar's Legion grows through aid,
training and protection. Nemesis—Caesar's created brother from Chapter One—
commands another legion and traps him in a meeting hall. The chapter ends with
his companions arriving to rescue him. It does not yet resolve the fight or
state the later fates of everyone present.

9. QUESTIONS THE CHAPTER LEAVES OPEN

Can current characters obtain Aura, and through whom? Does a transferred gift
carry Aura only or alter the recipient's existing Oasis? What determines each
recipient's expression? Can Terror repair living tissue? What exactly is
Venga's force and does it correspond to the earlier Hue? What is Shinichi's
wolf-like manifestation? Does Caesar Wind require a spoken name? What are the
ranges, energy costs, resistance checks and persistence rules? These remain
research questions; none should be filled with guessed mechanics.
