## Text

Adelram: Croco posts an award image of a blue, aquatic-looking creature with red eyes. It has no readable species or element label, so its exact power is unresolved. Croco later includes Adelram alongside Loki as having a common one-shell Slug Blaster. The creature's blue color is not sufficient to name it a Water Slug.
Kaizen and Arlo: spin requests appear, but no completed creature award is shown. The GIF response to Kaizen is not a grant. Other players' hypothetical species and combinations do not establish ownership.

The one-shell capacity applies to these awarded common blasters; it does not establish the capacity of every future device. Mimic Slugs copying the owner's powers are an explicitly described rare species, but no player here is awarded one. YellowCell, all-stone, Burpy and other proposed Slug combinations remain plans or comparisons. Bond growth and rarity do not supply an automatic instant mastery rule, reproduction exploit or limitless copying of council-locked stones.
