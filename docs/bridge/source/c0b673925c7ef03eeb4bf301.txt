## Text

K says eligible powers can go directly into a recipient's Outside System inventory or be implanted into the body/soul of someone without a system. The donor may give the original away or create a separate copy while retaining the original. Recipient consent and capacity to handle the ability are required; an unsuitable transfer can harm them. A permitted transfer is not proof of mastery or a copy of the donor's total power level.

Explicitly permitted: all eight Fundamental Manipulations, White Flames, Black Flames, Lyra's Magic, Jinka's abilities and other powers without exclusive ownership restrictions. Explicitly restricted: Grey Flames, all five of Loki's YellowCell flames, Judgement Day, Primal Staff abilities, Multiverse Stone abilities, the Dragon x2 billion Ultimate Form and its exclusive abilities. Sealed powers remain inaccessible unless Loki authorizes release.

K also explicitly forbids transferring Loki's YellowCell, merging it with another person's YellowCell, or granting it to someone without YellowCell. The stated reasons are Null's possession rules, the system's prohibition on personally bound powers and Loki's unique acquisition. A recipient already having YellowCell or an Outside System does not remove the restriction. This is a ruling on Loki's transfer route; do not infer that all independently acquired YellowCells are impossible.

K sends all-eight-manipulation trade requests to Adelram and Dargen. Dargen accepts and L completes the transfer at 11:04 UTC: all eight are registered and available to learn/control. Track this as Dargen's personal acquisition, separate from earlier access through a merge. The set remains Shadow, Spatial, Energy, Existence, Matter, Reality, Soul and Time; possessing them does not automatically create White/Grey/Judgement Day flames.

Adelram says accept, but X mistakenly presses decline. The source then confirms that the request expired and must be resent. Loki's final recorded action asks K to resend; no new request or successful acceptance has yet been resolved. Do not award Adelram all eight or complete his Energy quest. Loki also asks K to transfer to Kaizen through the no-system route, but no implant or acquisition is resolved. No transfer of White Flames, Black Flames, Lyra or Jinka to a new recipient is shown here.
